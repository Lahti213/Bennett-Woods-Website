Why Bennett Woods

Suburban calm, minutes from the city

Bennett Woods sits in the heart of Smyrna: quiet enough for evening walks under the trees, close enough that Atlanta, the Silver Comet Trail, and downtown Smyrna are a short drive away.

Beautiful setting

Mature trees, well-kept yards, and green space create a peaceful place you'll look forward to coming home to.

Strong community

Block parties, Garden Club events, and neighbors who stop to chat make Bennett Woods feel like home from day one.

Prime location

Quick access to I-285 plus nearby shopping, dining, parks, and trails. Everything you need is close.

Homes with character

Well-built houses with charm, cared for by owners who take real pride in their properties.

Neighbors look out

A close-knit street culture where people know each other, backed by Smyrna's own police and fire departments.

Great for families

Strong schools and homes priced within reach for first-time buyers, young professionals, and growing families.

Life here

A neighborhood made for living

One of the best things about Bennett Woods is how walkable and friendly it is. Any time of day you'll see neighbors walking dogs, kids riding bikes, and families cruising by in golf carts. The golf cart culture here is real, and it adds to the relaxed feel of the place.

Evening walks are a neighborhood tradition. Whether you're out with the dog, pushing a stroller, or just enjoying the shade, you'll run into friendly faces. It's the kind of place where kids play together and everyone looks out for each other.

Many residents are first-time buyers and young professionals who found what they were looking for here: safe streets, great schools, friendly neighbors, and home prices that leave room to invest in the future.

Schools

Zoned for a top-rated elementary

King Springs Elementary School

Bennett Woods families are served by King Springs, ranked #86 among Georgia's public elementary schools by Niche, with a 13:1 student-teacher ratio. 67% of students score proficient or above in reading and 58% in math.

Parents praise the caring staff, dedicated principal, and an active PTA. After-school programs include chess, art, music, and science, and a gifted program serves 14% of students.

"We feel a strong sense of community… our son has excelled and looks forward to going to school every day."

Safety

Served by Smyrna's own first responders

Bennett Woods is inside the City of Smyrna, which runs its own police and fire departments, so help is close by.

Smyrna Fire Department

Fire Station 2 sits right on Concord Road, minutes from the neighborhood. Its firefighters and paramedics respond to fires, medical emergencies, and rescues around the clock.

Smyrna Police Department

Officers patrol the neighborhood and practice community policing. Keep porch lights on in the evening and report anything suspicious; neighbors working together keep Bennett Woods safe.
