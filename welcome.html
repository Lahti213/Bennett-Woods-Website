New neighbor guide

Welcome to Bennett Woods!

We're glad you're here. This page covers the practical stuff for your first few weeks, plus the best ways to meet the neighbors.

Getting set up

Your move-in checklist

Bennett Woods is inside Smyrna city limits, so water, sewer and trash all come from the City of Smyrna.

  1. Water, sewer & trash service

    Apply with City of Smyrna Utility Services before move-in. One application covers water, sewer and sanitation. There's no same-day service, so give them a few business days.

    • (678) 631-5338
    • water@smyrnaga.gov
    • 2800 King Street · Mon–Fri, 8–5
    Service applications

  2. Find your trash day

    Curbside trash and recycling are picked up Tuesday through Friday, depending on your street. Yard waste goes out Mondays in paper bags (30 lb max, up to 10 bags). There's no yard waste pickup during holiday weeks.

    • Sanitation: (770) 431-2850
    Look up your pickup day

  3. Electricity

    Depending on your address, your power comes from either Cobb EMC or Georgia Power. You can't choose. Ask the sellers which one they used, or check with both companies.

    Cobb EMC Georgia Power

  4. Natural gas

    Atlanta Gas Light owns the pipes but doesn't sell gas. You pick a certified gas marketer and compare their rates. The Georgia Public Service Commission keeps the official list.

    Georgia PSC

  5. Enroll at school

    Bennett Woods is zoned for King Springs Elementary. Enrollment goes through the Cobb County School District, which also confirms zoning for your address.

    Cobb County Schools King Springs

  6. Update your address

    Update your driver's license and voter registration. Register your vehicle with the Cobb County Tax Commissioner; new Georgia residents generally have 30 days.

    Georgia DDS My Voter Page

Last checked November 2026. City schedules and providers change, so confirm details with the city or provider.

Meet the neighbors

Ways to get connected

Take an evening walk

Seriously, it's the fastest way in. Dog walkers, stroller pushers and golf carts are out most evenings, and people stop to chat.

Join the Garden Club

The club looks after all three entrances and runs the chili cook-off, wreath workshop and floral classes. No green thumb required.
